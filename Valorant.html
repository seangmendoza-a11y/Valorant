What is Valorant?

Valorant is a free-to-play, 5v5 tactical first-person shooter developed and published by Riot Games.
Riot Games where two teams switch between attacking and defending objectives across high-stakes rounds.
Players select unique Agents, using tactical abilities and gunplay to win 13 rounds for victory.
