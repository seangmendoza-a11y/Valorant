HOW TO PLAY VALORANT

Basic VALORANT Mechanics

VALORANT is a 5v5 tactical team game. One team attacks and the other defends. The attackers generally try to plant the Spike, while defenders try to stop the plant or defuse the Spike. If all players on one team are eliminated, the other team can also win the round.

Agent Roles

Role Main Job Simple Explanation
Duelist Entry / fighting Usually leads attacks and creates opportunities through aggressive abilities.
Initiator Gather information / set up fights Helps the team enter sites by revealing, distracting, or disrupting enemies.
Controller Control areas Uses abilities to block enemy vision and make areas safer for the team.
Sentinel Defense / protection Protects areas, watches flanks, and helps the team hold territory.
